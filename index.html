Nest Egg Compound Interest Paycheck Wheel

Which one do you need?

Four simple tools for thinking about your money. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere, nothing is saved.

Retirement Nest Egg

Work backwards from the yearly income you want to the portfolio you need to fund it.

  • 4% rule, with an adjustable withdrawal rate
  • Inflation-adjusted withdrawals each year
  • Optional income tax for any province or territory
  • Tells you if the money lasts — or runs out
Open calculator

Compound Interest

Watch a starting balance plus monthly contributions snowball over the years.

  • Monthly contributions and any compounding schedule
  • Splits the result into contributions vs. interest
  • Shows the effective annual yield you're really earning
Open calculator

Ontario Paycheck

See what actually lands in your account after tax, CPP and EI come off a salary.

  • Monthly, semi-monthly, bi-weekly or weekly pay
  • Federal and Ontario brackets shown step by step
  • Includes CPP2, surtax and the health premium
Open calculator

The Options Wheel

A map of the wheel strategy on RY — every branch from selling the first put to the shares being called away.

  • Both legs: cash-secured puts and covered calls
  • What happens on each side of the strike
  • One full turn worked through in dollars
